# VPN — quick card (use this one)

_Space: IT-SD · Owner: Kamil Wójcik · Last edited: 2025-09-20_

Short version for people who just want to connect:

- Client: **FortiClient**
- Gateway: `vpn.nordfarm.example`, port 443
- Login: Windows username + password
- Second factor: Authenticator app (push) — the old key fobs still work until end of 2025
- Not working? Call the servicedesk, ext. 4411, or open a ticket on NordDesk.

Full instructions with screenshots are on the "VPN setup" page.
